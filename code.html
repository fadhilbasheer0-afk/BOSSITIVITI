Our Story

"BOSSITIVITI was never a business idea for me. It was a response."

For years, people would stop me and ask, "What perfume are you wearing?" That fragrance was NB THOUSAND—my personal choice, my identity, my silent signature. I realized something powerful in those moments: a fragrance can change how people see you... and how you see yourself.

From NB THOUSAND, we expanded the vision to NB MILLION, NB BILLION, NB TRILLION, NB OCTILLION, and NB DECILLION—each representing a higher level of presence, power, and personality.
